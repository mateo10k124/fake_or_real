How to Identify Fake News

Five simple ways to verify information.

01. Check the Source

Make sure the website or author is trustworthy.

02. Check the Date

Old news can be shared as if it were new.

03. Compare Sources

Look for the same information on other reliable websites.

04. Look for Evidence

Check whether the information includes verifiable facts.

05. Think Before Sharing

Do not share information you have not verified.
